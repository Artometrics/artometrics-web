Model Drift, Quantified
A four-month audit of 48 production forecasting models found most degradation surfaces long before the first quarterly review.
By the Artometrics Research Desk—2 August 2026

Across forty-eight production deployments audited between January and April, drift showed up faster than teams expected — and almost never where their monitoring was pointed.

"The correlation was real. The causal story we told about it was not."

Feature staleness, not model age, carried most of the predictive signal for when a system would need retraining. Retraining cadence alone explained under a fifth of the variance we measured.

Teams that logged predictions at inference time caught drift three times faster than teams relying on weekly batch sampling — a gap large enough to change how audits should be scheduled going forward.
