# Draft scaffold (Psychonomics cover)

## Working dek

Economics manages the house. Psychonomics asks who manages the soul of the house — and what that architecture costs. Julius Caesar scaled biography into state. Beyoncé scaled self into firm. Michael Jackson scaled self into **product**: the catalog *was* the body, Neverland *was* the household myth, and the public was invited to consume both. This profile quantifies the catalog immortality and faces the dual ledger without turning into a fan war or a true-crime special.

## Research question

What does it cost a culture when one artist becomes the product line — and how do commercial immortality and published harm claims share one balance sheet?
