# Draft — Narrative Economics field guide (feedstock)

*Status: drafting · Artometrics method chapter*

## Dek

Everyone says “the narrative changed.” Almost nobody can define a narrative as an economic object. Robert Shiller’s narrative economics treats popular stories as contagious — with infection rates, mutations, and burnout — that move markets and institutions. This is the briefing note for culture workers tired of vibes language.

## Research question

What is a measurable economic narrative, how does it spread, and how should operators brief with it without becoming grifters?

## Definitions

- **Economic narrative:** a contagious story that can affect decisions (spend, invest, cancel, hire, silence).  
- **Contagion:** growth in tellings that behaves like an epidemic curve (search, news volume, social).  
- **Mutation:** slogan variants that keep the story alive (*box office poison*, *everyone knew*, *too big to fail*).  
- **Burnout:** decline in new infections even if the underlying fact remains true.

## Mechanism

Stories compete for attention like strains. The fittest are simple, moralized, and easy to retell. Markets and institutions do not need the story to be true to price it; they need it to be **common knowledge** that others are acting on it. Preference falsification (Kuran) is the silence twin: private disbelief with public compliance until a cascade threshold.

## Four worked cases

1. **Crypto** — “digital gold” → “tulip” flip; measure slogan volume, not theology.  
2. **Pandemic** — reopen / forever / origin stories as competing epidemics.  
3. **Meme stocks** — retail story as coordination device.  
4. **Celebrity ruin** — “why not sooner?” itself becomes a contagious frame (bridge to Protection Threshold).

## Monday brief template

1. Name the narrative in one sentence.  
2. Show the curve (Trends / news hits).  
3. State which decision it is distorting.  
4. Propose a falsifier (what evidence would kill the story).

## Limits

Narrative volume ≠ truth. Magazine charts of contagion are instruments, not verdicts. Pair with Protection Threshold when the story is institutional silence.
