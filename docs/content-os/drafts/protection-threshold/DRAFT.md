# Draft — The Protection Threshold (feedstock)

*Status: drafting · Peg: BBC Jared Leto documentary Jul 2026 · Legal: allegation/denial; public box office*

## Dek

The BBC’s *Jared Leto: Hollywood’s Dark Secret* put a familiar public question back on the table: **why not sooner?** Artometrics asks a colder one: **what was he still worth?** This report tests whether institutional protection tracks commercial and risk thresholds — using Leto’s dual film/music books, Weinstein Company’s pre-2017 decline, Bryan Singer’s *Bohemian Rhapsody* credit without rehab, and broadcaster cases (Savile, Brand).

## Research question

Can we construct a quantitative marker for how much forward commercial or institutional value someone can lose before Hollywood or a broadcaster stops protecting them — and does the Leto moment fit?

## Working positions

- Leto 2020s lead/tentpole attachments look commercially impaired.  
- Music may be a second, healthier book — dual-book hypothesis.  
- TWC was already declining before Oct 2017.  
- *BoRhap* does **not** mean Singer was “back.”  
- Brand may falsify pure flop-first.

## Limitations

Allegations ≠ convictions. Flops may precede, follow, or be unrelated to exposés. Selection bias: we study the eventually exposed. Journalists also time stories.
