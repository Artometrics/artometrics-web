## 1. One-sentence thesis (to test, not preach)

Institutions often look “brave” when they finally drop someone — but the timing may track **commercial and institutional risk**, not only moral discovery. This report asks whether we can measure a **protection threshold**: how much franchise value, box-office run rate, or institutional usefulness someone can lose before silence becomes more expensive than exposure.

**Core claim candidates (pick after data):**

1. **Cheap-to-drop hypothesis:** Public reckoning clusters when the subject’s *forward* economic value is already impaired.  
2. **Dual-book hypothesis:** Film can flop while music (or social/influencer revenue) still props protection — Leto may be the test case.  
3. **Death-as-protection hypothesis:** Jimmy Savile is the null case — if you never become cheap while alive, the institution may never pay the cost.  
4. **Success-is-not-rehab hypothesis:** Bryan Singer’s *Bohemian Rhapsody* credit proves DGA rules and sunk shoot costs, not that he was “back.”

## 2. What just happened (Leto peg — named facts)

From BBC News / *Jared Leto: Hollywood's Dark Secret* (Jessica Sartenaer, Alice McShane, Mike Radford, Kirstie Brewer):

- **10 women** spoke to the BBC; **9** for the first time publicly in this film.  
- **4 women** allege **criminal sexual conduct** when they were teenagers (Isabel, Alex, Clara, Etta — some names changed). Alleged window **2002–2016**; Leto then in his 30s–40s, now 54.  
- Allegations include: assault in a motel bathroom at 17; threat of sexual assault at 19; sex in California at 17 (statutory rape under CA law as framed by BBC); sexually explicit calls at 16; a 14-year-old’s account of a sexual comment + attempted backstage pull.  
- BBC says it corroborated some accounts with contemporaneous friends/family, photos, messages; two long-time band workers describe discomfort with Leto’s interactions with teenage girls.  
- **Prior airing of smoke:** Allie Teilz (2025) + Air Mail piece (9 women, Jun 2025); BBC counts **120+** separate online allegations over years.  
- **Leto statement after publication:** denies sexual assault; calls claims “categorically false.” He did not respond to BBC before publication (per BBC), then issued denial.

**Guardrails for our piece:** allegation / denial / sourcing language throughout. No courtroom conviction claimed. No graphic reenactment. Link BBC as primary secondary source; do not launder unverified forum posts as facts.

## 3. Research question

**Can we construct a quantitative marker for “how much a celebrity can lose before Hollywood (or a broadcaster) stops protecting them” — and does the Leto moment fit that pattern?**

Sub-questions:

1. Relative to budget and genre comps, how bad is Leto’s 2020s film book (*Morbius*, *Tron: Ares*, *Masters of the Universe*, etc.)?  
2. Does Thirty Seconds to Mars still clear a different (healthier) commercial book — touring, streams, catalog — that would delay a film-side write-down?  
3. Was Weinstein / TWC already in measurable decline before Oct 2017?  
4. Does Singer’s *Bohemian Rhapsody* success contradict the cheap-to-drop hypothesis — or does the timeline (fired mid-shoot → Atlantic 2019 → Red Sonja collapse → quiet fringe return) support a sharper version?  
5. What do Savile and Brand teach when the “studio” is a public broadcaster and the product is trust, not opening weekend?

## 5. Data plan (Artometrics-shaped)

### A. Film commercial book (primary charts)

For each subject with film credits:

- Title, year, reported budget (studio/press), WW gross (Box Office Mojo / The Numbers)  
- Derived: **gross / budget**, **estimated theatrical break-even proxy** (e.g. 2–2.5× reported budget — label as editorial index, not accounting truth)  
- Role weight: lead vs supporting (Leto *MOTU* Skeletor ≠ *Tron* top-line)  
- Rolling **3-film forward value** index before exposé date vs after  

**Leto film slate to code first:** *Morbius* (2022), *Tron: Ares* (2025), *Masters of the Universe* (2026), plus comps (*House of Gucci*, *Haunted Mansion*, *The Little Things*) with role notes.

### B. Music / non-film book (Leto dual test)

- 30STM album certifications / chart peaks  
- Tour routing (O2 / arena dates already announced per BBC)  
- Streaming proxy if available (Spotify for Artists public monthly listeners — observed only)  

**Hypothesis:** jokes about the music “sucking” ≠ commercial death; need numbers.

### C. Studio / firm health (Weinstein)

- MarketWatch / LA Times / Forbes contemporaneous reporting on TWC decline  
- Highest-grossing TWC title per year curve (already sketched in 2017 coverage)  
- Debt figures (~$500M+ financing citations) — cite press, not invent ledgers  

### D. Event timeline layer (all cases)

Columns: `first_public_allegation_cluster`, `major_investigative_piece`, `institutional_severance` (fired / dropped by agency / show cancelled), `criminal_inflection`, `commercial_state_at_severance` (up / flat / down — coded with rules).

### E. What we will **not** pretend to measure

- Private NDAs, hush money, or “how many people knew” as a count  
- Moral desert or guilt  
- Exact studio P&L without filings
