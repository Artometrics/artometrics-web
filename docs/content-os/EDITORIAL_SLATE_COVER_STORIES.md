## What you actually like (the pattern)

You’re not chasing random celebrity drama. You’re chasing **systems where desire, status, money, and institutions delay truth** — then asking whether the delay can be *measured*.

Recurring engines across your asks (Apocrypha, Protection Threshold, Playboy, DiCaprio, Leto, Savile, Brand, Psychonomics):

| Engine | Plain English |
|--------|----------------|
| **Protection economics** | When does silence stop being profitable? |
| **Dual books** | Public brand vs private behavior; film flops vs music still touring |
| **Age × access** | Who gets proximity to power while still forming — and who markets that proximity |
| **Institutional afterlife** | What a brand/broadcaster does after the founder/monster is gone (Hef, Savile, BBC) |
| **Psychonomics** | Inner architecture of power scaled into firms, catalogs, lyrics, armies |
| **Discipline translation** | Narrative economics, sociobiology, etc. — expensive insight made outreach-ready |
| **Apocrypha reflex** | Stories that *feel* like cover exposés (sometimes fiction, sometimes report) |

**Not cookie-cutter TidyTuesday.** Those stay as the data gym. This slate is the **magazine cover wall**.
