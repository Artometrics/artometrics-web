ARTS — What a Century of Anime Titles Reveals About the Industry

This MyAnimeList dataset covers 13,631 unique anime titles spanning 1917 to 2019 — a century-scale archive tracing the medium from prewar theater shorts to the standardized streaming machine of the 2010s. The key interpretive move here is separating catalog reality (what actually got made) from fan-canon popularity (what people actually watched and rated).
