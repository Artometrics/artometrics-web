How damage sticks to deer antlers year after year.

Injure one point on the rack → callus → shed. Next year: an ectopic branch at the same address. The bone that held the scar is gone, so the map has to live somewhere else — then get written into new growth decisions.

Source: Michael Levin on the Bubenik trophic-memory archive (labeled multi-year antler sets, later CT-scanned). Primary write-up: thoughtforms.life / trophic memory.
