WHAT THIS FILE CANNOT TELL YOU

Community-cleaned TidyTuesday snapshots are not live APIs. Missing values, spelling variants, and week-of-export coverage limits apply. Merged tables may fan out or duplicate rows when join keys are imperfect.

Findings describe the file on hand—structural signals about Netflix title rows through 2021, not today's live catalog in every territory.

WHAT TO TAKE AWAY

Median duration climbed from 41.0 to 98.0 while Movie remained the most common type. A handful of long outliers—Bandersnatch at 312 among them—and a 38 percent duration concentration in the top five rows show how uneven a "library" can be once you stop counting titles and start summing length.
